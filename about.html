The Architect Behind the Code

My story didn’t start in a lab or a lecture hall.
It started in the 6th grade, in front of a glowing CRT, teaching myself MS‑DOS and QBasic out of pure obsession.

That curiosity turned into decades of tearing systems apart, rebuilding them, and learning every layer of the stack the hard way — through grit, repetition, and real‑world problem‑solving.

I moved from procedural scripts into full object‑oriented development across Java, C#, C++, Python, and more.

Graphics design, web design, logic design — I learned them because I needed them, not because someone assigned them.

I spent two years interning with the Charles County Board of Education, completing Honors Java and AP Computer Science with distinction.

From there, my path got stranger and wider:

  • managing high‑traffic game servers
  • contributing to FDIC bank fraud investigations
  • working customer‑facing roles that sharpened my leadership
  • running FauxC, where I rebuilt PCs from the motherboard up

Every chapter added precision, empathy, and resilience to my toolkit.
Every challenge sharpened the fox.
